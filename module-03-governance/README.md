# Module 03: Governance
 
## Who Votes, and How Voting Power Is Weighted
 
Governance power in SHx is simple: one token equals one vote, measured by the account's balance at the moment of a snapshot (a specific ledger defined when a proposal opens), truncated to an integer with no fractional votes. There is no mandatory staking or delegation by default; any holder with a balance at the snapshot can participate directly.
 
## The Mechanism: ManageData, Not a Soroban Contract
 
This was already confirmed in depth in Module 02, but it is worth restating here since it is the core of this module: although Stronghold's own governance documentation references a Contract ID, that contract is the standard SAC (SEP-41), automatically generated by the protocol, with no voting function of any kind. Real voting is entirely classic Stellar mechanics:
 
- **Operation 1:** `manageData(name: "SHX::PROP:<n>", value: "<position>")`, e.g. `name: "SHX::PROP:7"`, `value: "FOR,40"`
- **Operation 2 (optional):** `manageData(name: "SHX::PROP:<n>", value: "")`, clears the entry to avoid an ongoing ledger reserve cost
- The account's **last vote** within the voting window is the one that counts; resubmitting changes the vote
This is exactly what is implemented in `ShxGovernance.buildCastVoteOperation()` and `buildClearVoteOperation()` in `stronghold_flutter_sdk`.
 
## The Ethereum Side: Snapshot, a Completely Different Mechanism
 
Holders of SHx's ERC-20 representation on Ethereum do not vote via ManageData, since that is exclusive to Stellar. They vote through **Snapshot** (`snapshot.box/#/s:strongholdco.eth`), an off-chain signature system (EIP-712) widely used in DAO governance. It is important not to conflate the two mechanisms: they are different platforms and signature protocols, connected only by representing the same underlying economic voting power on the same token.
 
## Proposal History: What Is Confirmed and What Is Not
 
Stronghold documents 7 governance proposals in total. Three were researched in depth:
 
| # | Topic | Outcome | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| #3 | Opening StrongholdNET to a public developer console (SHX-settled APIs) | Passed | High, confirmed in official documentation |
| #5 | "SHx Governance Snapshot," a proposed Soroban contract unifying Stellar and Ethereum votes into a single record, connected to a broader governance hub | Passed | High on the proposal itself; **on-chain deployment status not confirmed** |
| #6 | lSHX ("Vote Locked SHx"), allows locking SHx in exchange for greater voting power, non-decaying while locked | Passed | High on the proposal itself; **on-chain deployment status not confirmed** |
 
Proposals #1, #2, #4, and #7 exist (numbering runs through 7), but their specific content was not researched in depth here, so they are not detailed in this module to avoid stating unverified content as fact.
 
## Open Questions
 
Both the Governance Snapshot contract (#5) and lSHX (#6) fall into the same category: approved by vote, on-chain deployment status unconfirmed. This calls for the same kind of investigation already carried out for the escrow contract (finding the real Contract ID, inspecting it via `stellar contract inspect`), pending a future session and already tracked as a shared open item in the SDK's roadmap.
